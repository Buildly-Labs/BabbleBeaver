Privacy Policy

BabbleBeaver — Effective November 25, 2026

Micro AI platform for LLM switching, dataset preparation, and model deployment.

This Privacy Policy explains what information BabbleBeaver collects, how it is used, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use BabbleBeaver, we may collect:

  • Account email and name
  • API keys for LLM providers (encrypted at rest)
  • Datasets and model training data you upload
  • Usage logs

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the application
  • To authenticate you and maintain your session
  • To improve features and fix bugs
  • To respond to support requests

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

BabbleBeaver may interact with the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic, OpenAI, and other LLM providers you configure
  • Docker Hub (model deployment)

Each service has its own privacy policy. We only connect to services you explicitly authorize.

4. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored locally on your device where possible. When server storage is used, it is hosted on secured infrastructure. We use industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest where applicable.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at support@buildly.io to exercise these rights.

7. Children’s Privacy

BabbleBeaver is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the effective date above.
